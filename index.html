Property restoration · Featured project

Norgard Restoration

Before: Jon left a larger restoration company to start his own. He had the expertise and the clients — but no systems. Jobs ran on a paper docket, with photos and signed documents in folders on a desktop, and QuickBooks on the side. In his words: “messy and not efficient.”

What we built: one system for the whole job. A website that turns enquiries into leads. A job pipeline from inspection to paid. Electronic work authorizations and signatures. And a trades portal, so the people on site can upload photos and notes from their phones — organized by job and room, not by memory.

Where it stands: the system is live — website, job management, and signatures — and Jon’s team runs jobs through it. QuickBooks and Xactimate integrations are on the roadmap; the first version focused on getting the daily work out of paper and folders and into one place.

Before you call

What does it cost?

It depends on what we’re building — and that’s exactly what the free consultation is for. Once we agree on the scope, you get clear pricing in writing before any work starts. There’s no separate build fee: the build is included, and the monthly retainer covers hosting, monitoring, fixes, and ongoing changes.

How long does it take?

Most systems are working within weeks, not months. We start with the smallest useful version, ship it, and improve it with real feedback — instead of spending months on a big launch that misses the mark.

Do I own the system?

Yes. It’s built for your business, documented, and set up so you can operate it independently. No lock-in.

I already have software I like. Do I have to replace it?

No. We build around the tools you already use and connect them, so the new system fits your business instead of disrupting it.

What happens after launch?

That’s the monthly retainer: monitoring, fixes, and ongoing changes as your business evolves. We keep iterating until the system fits — and keep fitting it as you grow.
